FAQ

Questions people ask

Who is it for?

Freelancers, consultants, makers, Etsy and Shopify sellers, coaches, trades and side hustles that want simple cash-basis books. The Job-Cost tracker is for contractors, builders, remodelers and agencies that bill by project.

Who is it not for?

Businesses that need double-entry accounting, payroll, inventory valuation or sales-tax filing. Use accounting software or a bookkeeper for those.
